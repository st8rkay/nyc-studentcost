01 / 06 · THE HOOK

01 · Context

A budget is more than the little things.

Students hear that cutting everyday purchases will solve money stress.

But the biggest costs are often necessities: housing, food, and transportation.

What happens when essentials take up most of the space?

Start with household expenses across the NYC metro area. These are not estimates of student spending, but they show the wider cost context.
